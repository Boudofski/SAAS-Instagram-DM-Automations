What Meta Business Partner Status Means

Meta Business Partner status is awarded only to companies that meet strict eligibility and performance criteria defined by Meta. These criteria focus on platform compliance, quality of integrations, data protection standards, and real-world usage across active business accounts.

Based on the screenshots from Meta Partner Center, our status is clearly shown as Member and Compliant, confirming that LinktoDM currently meets all required benchmarks.
