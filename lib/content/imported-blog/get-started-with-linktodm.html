LinktoDM offers a completely free experience to get started, with no need to worry about costs. Enjoy a 3-month free trial of our premium features, and even after that, you can continue using LinktoDM without a paid plan.

Before getting started to LinktoDM you'll need an Instagram account switched to Professional account and connected to a Facebook page.

Here's how to switch your Instagram account to Creator Account.

After switching to Creator account, connect your Instagram account to a Facebook page. If you don't have a Facebook page, create one.

Once you make sure Instagram account is connected to Facebook page, you can create your account at linktodm.com

Select 'Get Started' to begin signup process

Continue with your Google Account

After setting up your Google account, proceed to log in to your Facebook account, which is linked to your Instagram account, in order to configure automation settings.

Choose the Facebook page associated with your Instagram account and grant all necessary permissions and you're done with the sign-up process.

You can now create automation by clicking 'create' button.

Enter the 'Automation Name', trigger keywords, auto-reply message for the comment box, and DM message with links. Once the rules are configured, click on 'Create and Publish' to start the automation.
