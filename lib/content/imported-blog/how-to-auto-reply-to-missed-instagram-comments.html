What Are Old or Missed Instagram Comments?

Old or missed Instagram comments are interactions that you didn’t notice or reply to when they first appeared. They usually happen when:

  • Notifications get buried under new alerts
  • A post receives too many comments at once
  • Someone comments days or weeks later
  • You’re not actively checking older posts
  • Manual tracking becomes difficult as your account grows

Over time, these unanswered comments can quietly pile up, leading to lost conversations, lower engagement, and missed opportunities to connect with followers or potential customers.

How to Edit Your Backtracking Keywords

When using backtracking, LinktoDM scans past comments based only on the keywords you originally set as triggers. However, as your campaign grows, you might realize that people use different words or phrases that should also activate your Auto-DMs. In such cases, you can simply edit or add new trigger keywords so the system recognizes more relevant comments during backtracking. Updating these keywords ensures your automation stays accurate, captures more interactions, and responds to a wider range of user messages. The steps below will guide you on how to quickly modify your trigger words.

  1. Step 1: Head to the Automation Dashboard.

  2. Step 2: Tap the three dots on the automation you wish to manage, and Click on ‘Edit’ option.

  3. Step 3: Add more keywords from the Triggers section.

  4. Step 4: Click ‘Publish’.

After adding new keywords, save changes by clicking on the ‘Publish’ button at the top right corner.
Now when you start backtracking, the newly added trigger words will appear in the keyword list.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How far back does LinktoDM's backtracking go?
LinktoDM’s backtracking feature can automatically scan and reply to Instagram comments that are up to 7 days old. This means even if you missed comments from the past week, the system can still detect them and send your auto-replies, helping you recover lost engagement and continue conversations without manual checking.
Does backtracking work with private accounts?
No, backtracking does not work with private Instagram accounts. However, LinktoDM makes this easy to fix — during the account linking process, you’ll be guided to switch your Instagram account to a Creator or Professional (Business) account. This only takes a few minutes and is required for automation features like auto-replies and backtracking to function properly.
Is using backtracking safe for my account?
Yes, backtracking is safe to use as long as it’s set up correctly. LinktoDM follows Instagram’s automation guidelines and only responds based on the triggers and limits you choose. Since you control the reply messages, keywords, and frequency, the automation behaves naturally and avoids spam-like activity. When used responsibly, backtracking simply helps you manage old and missed comments more efficiently without risking your account.
Can I change my auto-reply message later?
Yes — you can update your auto-reply messages anytime. The process is just as simple as editing your trigger keywords. Follow the same steps shown in the “How to Edit Your Backtracking Keywords” section, but instead of changing keywords, you can edit your existing replies or add new reply messages. This lets you quickly adjust your automation whenever your campaign, offer, or tone changes.
Does backtracking work on multiple posts?
Backtracking works on all eligible posts connected to your automation. Once enabled, LinktoDM scans comments across your selected Instagram content and detects old or missed comments automatically, so you don’t have to check each post manually. This ensures consistent engagement and replies across your posts instead of limiting automation to just one.
