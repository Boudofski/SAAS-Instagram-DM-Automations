# AP3K marketing review — 5 November 2026

## Scope and evidence
Reviewed existing product context, public pricing component, plan-limit source, billing presentation, localization provider and consent-gated Google Analytics helper. Installed the complete upstream marketing workflow collection at commit 88c79f6528a9347420e57ceb1fff9a60de651768. This is a source/UI review, not a customer study or a measured conversion experiment.

## Applied improvement
The pricing page defines actions but required visitors to calculate capacity themselves. Added localized, derived examples: one public reply plus one DM costs two actions. Free therefore covers up to 250 such pairs, Pro 2,500 and Business 10,000 when no other actions are used. Explicitly explain that opening messages and follow-ups reduce that capacity. Keep this explanation out of the paid-only billing modal. Preserve price, checkout, enforcement, metadata, consent and customer data contracts.

## Priorities
1. Activation: retain the existing one-post, one-keyword, one-link launch-kit funnel. Measure registration → connected account → active campaign → first successful send → repeat use. A stored connection or CTA click is not successful delivery.
2. Revenue: compare activated cohorts with actual paid subscriptions and retained use. Establish the baseline before expanding Free limits or discounting plans. 500 actions is an initial experiment budget, not 500 contacts; competitor comparisons must use matching units.
3. Acquisition: concentrate on comment-to-DM and ManyChat-alternative intent; keep matching content and useful demos. More articles without evidence of demand or activation do not establish a ranking or revenue advantage.
4. Trust: collect permission-based customer proof from pilots; use only verifiable outcomes. Do not invent customer logos, results, scarcity or superiority claims.
5. Lifecycle: audit existing opt-in and lifecycle triggers before adding sequences. Do not automatically send email, launch ads or enroll historic addresses from this installation.

## Measurement needed
No current visitor-to-signup, activation, paid-conversion or retention baseline was established here. Evaluate the pricing clarification over comparable acquisition cohorts; monitor signup completion, activation and paid conversion, not just pricing clicks. Segment by language and source only where consent and sufficient sample size permit. Do not claim uplift without observed results.

## Deferred deliberately
No blanket price/limit changes, mass content generation, outbound sends or ad spend. No second analytics framework. Existing SEO release remains intact. Select additional installed workflows against a concrete observed bottleneck rather than creating fifty disconnected marketing projects.
