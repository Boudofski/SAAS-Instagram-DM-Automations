# AP3K search audit and release — 23 October 2026

## Evidence and limits

- Google Search Console, 23 September–20 October: its 201 returned query/page rows show AP3K's observed clicks concentrated in brand and misspelling searches. Relevant queries such as `how to disconnect manychat from instagram` (10 impressions, average position 8.8) and `manychat alternative for instagram` (12 impressions, average position 37.8) had zero clicks in that period. This is a limited query/page sample, not a complete traffic or conversion report. Average position is not a live rank.
- Google's URL inspection: homepage, `/instagram-comment-to-dm`, an existing ManyChat article and the ManyChat disconnection doc were indexed. `/fr` and `/es` were crawled but not indexed as of their 19 October crawls, despite allowed robots, self-canonicals and sitemap inclusion. Google's result for `/blog/instagram-comment-to-dm-automation` was "URL is unknown to Google." Inspection is historical and may lag releases. None of these results proves a penalty.
- The OpenSEO crawl of 50 live URLs (23 October) found two cookie-policy titles duplicated between Spanish and Portuguese; seven description-length suggestions and two title-length suggestions. It did not report a site-wide crawl blocker in its limited sample. The 50 pages are not the entire 886-URL live sitemap.
- Local inventory before release: 357 bundled blog entries (167 imported archives), 886 generated sitemap URLs, with 16 articles having fully localized editorial copy. After release: 358 entries (166 imported archives), 893 unique planned URLs including six versions of one newly written guide and one English-only article. The other English-only articles are not multiplied into fake translated URLs.
- The local article-link inventory checked all structured post links and 1,119 imported archive link attributes; the only paths missing from the sitemap were functioning sign-in/help routes, which intentionally are not sitemap targets. This check does not guarantee that every external URL or product feature is current.

## Changes shipped in this release

1. Rewrote the existing indexed `/blog/best-manychat-alternative-for-instagram-dm-automation` URL with a product-specific comparison, current AP3K allowances from shared plan constants, explicit no-team-seats limitation, and a migration/delivery test. The old imported body advertised unrestricted usage and teammate access. Kept its original publication date and used the actual editorial update date for `dateModified` and sitemap `lastmod`.
2. Updated the indexed ManyChat disconnection documentation to distinguish disabling from removing the channel. ManyChat's [official help](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/15582498909724-How-to-remove-an-Instagram-account-from-Manychat) states that removal deletes associated Instagram contacts and must be performed in the desktop app. The guide links to that source and to an AP3K migration test.
3. Published `/instagram-automation-guide` with full, independent EN/FR/ES/DE/NL/PT body copy. Its five sections cover account prerequisites, trigger choice, usage counting, real delivery tests and troubleshooting. Every version has a self-canonical, reciprocal `hreflang`, page-language HTML, a matching `TechArticle` description, and direct footer navigation. Dutch links leading to English-only guides are labeled as English.
4. Added the original English-only `/blog/accessible-instagram-dm-links-mobile` guide on button labels, in-app browser testing, destination consistency, readability and outcome evidence. It links to existing maintained launch and tracking guides instead of duplicating them.
5. Corrected duplicate ES/PT cookie titles and the sampled title/description length issues. Added a factual `/llms.txt` as an optional discovery aid; it is not needed by Google and does not confer AI-search eligibility.
6. Added an IndexNow ownership file and an explicit URL-only submission script. This notifies participating engines such as Bing and Yandex when live canonical content changes; receipt does not mean indexing or ranking. It does not broadcast the entire sitemap or private routes.

## Remaining work ordered by measured opportunity

1. Inspect the French/Spanish homepages and representative localized content after the next Google crawl. Keep improving the actual page experience and natural in-language internal links; do not add translated URL variants to the imported English archive. Resubmit the sitemap in Google Search Console and inspect representative pages rather than treating a request for indexing as a guarantee.
2. Editorially review the 166 remaining imported archive articles. At least 26 older blog titles reference 2025 or earlier, and some third-party comparison pages quote changeable prices. Preserve historical dates where they are honest; revise an article only after checking its factual claims and the original product sources. Prioritize pages with actual non-brand impressions, then consolidate weak overlaps instead of mass-generating variants.
3. Track non-brand query/page impressions, indexed URLs, and actual leads on a 28-day cadence. Once those data reveal a winning topic, strengthen its own landing page and outreach, with permission from the external publisher when placing links. The present data do not support a ranking or revenue forecast.

## Search-engine notes

Google states that AI Overviews and AI Mode have no special file or schema requirement: indexed, useful, crawlable page content remains the basis. Bing and Yandex may receive IndexNow notifications; keep their webmaster properties and XML sitemap submissions maintained separately. An IndexNow 200/202 is only a submission receipt. See [Google's AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) and the [IndexNow protocol](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation).
