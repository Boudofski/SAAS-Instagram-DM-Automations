# AP3K search recovery — 9 November 2026

## Evidence
Search Console property: sc-domain:ap3k.com. Web search, finalized data. Equal 14-day windows: 9–22 October versus 24 October–6 November. The intervening day is excluded from both comparisons. Recent incomplete data was not used.

| Metric | Earlier | Recent |
|---|---:|---:|
| Homepage clicks | 458 | 112 |
| Homepage impressions | 1,780 | 655 |
| Homepage CTR | 25.73% | 17.10% |
| Homepage average position | 4.4 | 4.7 |
| Exact `ap3k` clicks | 204 | 57 |
| Exact `ap3k` impressions | 437 | 150 |
| Exact `ap3k` average position | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| `apx3k` clicks | 142 | 19 |
| `apx3k` impressions | 654 | 125 |
| `apx3k` average position | 2.9 | 1.7 |

The decline is primarily associated with fewer branded-query impressions, with a secondary CTR decline. These aggregates do not establish why search demand fell. They do not support a claim that AP3K lost its exact-brand ranking. Query totals exclude anonymized queries and should not be treated as complete site totals.

The free-instagram-chatbot article had 131 impressions and zero clicks in the recent window. Its reported query `fan engagement chatbot for instagram dms` had 101 impressions at average position 8.6. The established ManyChat comparison article received the reported ManyChat-alternative queries, mostly around positions 27–59; that remains a longer-term opportunity, not an immediate ranking promise.

## Crawl and index checks
Read-only crawl capped at 100 URLs from a sitemap inventory of 1,242. All sampled pages returned 200. No missing/duplicate titles or descriptions, non-self canonicals, invalid JSON-LD, or hreflang return-link issues were detected in the sample. This is not an audit of every URL or a Core Web Vitals result.

URL Inspection confirmed indexing and matching canonicals for the homepage, free-instagram-automation, instagram-comment-to-dm, instagram-dm-automation, manychat-alternative, the free chatbot and ManyChat comparison articles, and FR/ES/DE/AR homepages. `/compare/manychat` was unknown to Google; the maintained `/manychat-alternative` URL was indexed. Do not create a duplicate comparison page to solve that non-issue.

## Release
- Refresh three established article URLs: free-instagram-chatbot, how-to-set-up-auto-dm-on-instagram, is-it-safe-to-auto-dm-on-instagram.
- Replace generic promises with actual setup steps, examples, action accounting, limits and failure diagnosis.
- Remove a universal Facebook Page prerequisite and avoid guarantees of account safety, sales or delivery.
- Make the maintained chatbot, setup, comparison and safety guides the four featured blog entries, improving direct discovery from the archive.
- Retain existing article URLs and publication dates. Use 9 November only for the substantively revised articles; sitemap collection freshness derives from those dates.
- Keep English-only articles honestly canonicalized; do not advertise fabricated translations.

## Evaluation
Compare finalized 14-day windows after Google recrawls, separated into branded queries and non-brand queries. Track impressions, CTR, positions and actual signups/connected accounts where attribution is available. Do not interpret aggregate position changes as movement of one keyword. No recrawl, indexing, traffic or revenue improvement is claimed by this release.

Marketing follow-through: consistently use AP3K and ap3k.com in social captions and tutorials, and direct people to a useful campaign example. Search Console cannot establish social reach as the cause of lower branded demand, but code and metadata alone cannot restore that demand.
