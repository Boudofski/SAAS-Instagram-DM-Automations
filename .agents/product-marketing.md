# AP3K product marketing context

**Document version:** v3
**Last updated:** 2026-11-07
**Status:** Drafted from the product and observed data; audience/offer hypotheses need customer validation.

## Product
AP3K is a SaaS for Instagram professional accounts. It automates configured comment replies and DMs, with a flow editor, inbox, contact and campaign reporting. A store still owns its storefront and checkout. A creator still needs an offer and an audience.

## Initial audience
Prioritize creators who already get repeated requests for a resource or link. Secondary test: store owners with product/size/availability questions on Instagram. Exclude people seeking scraping, follower inflation, mass unsolicited DMs, or guaranteed sales.

## Positioning and offer
Launch one working comment-to-DM campaign: one post, one keyword, one useful link. The public launch kit contains complete scripts and a real-account test. Three optional emails help a prospect use it. Free/Pro/Business tiers exist; verify live pricing before publishing price-sensitive campaigns.

## Jobs and objections
Deliver the exact requested link without repeatedly typing it; make the next step clear; distinguish configured actions from actual downstream results. Explain professional account requirements, supported authorization, platform constraints, and testing. Never ask for Instagram passwords. Customers who need setup help should see one concrete next action.

## Competition
Direct category alternatives include ManyChat and LinktoDM. Manual replies and native saved replies are alternatives too. No claim that AP3K is universally cheaper, faster, officially endorsed, or more effective without current evidence.

## Voice and evidence
Direct, specific, helpful. Use “comment,” “keyword,” “DM,” “resource,” and “campaign.” Avoid invented ROI, ranking promises, fake scarcity, fake customers, and partner badges without proof. No verified customer quotes or conversion results were established in this release. Product demonstrations must be labeled as demonstrations.

## Goals
AP3K is already in use. The owner’s 2026-11-05 admin screenshot shows 165 registered accounts, 51 connections, 77 active automations and 61 successful sends that day. The owner uses AP3K for @boudofi. These are dated screenshot observations, not a current database query or verified paid-customer count. Prioritize activation and retention of existing signups alongside acquisition. The last-30-day snapshot shows 122 signups, 40 connected accounts and 13 accounts with a successful send; owner/test accounts are included and milestones are not strictly nested. Distinguish traffic, CTA clicks, registrations, stored connection states, sent messages, verified delivery, and revenue.

## Verified packaging (2026-11-05)
Source: `lib/plan-limits.ts` and `lib/billing-plans.ts`. Free: 500 actions/month, 1 Instagram account, 5 active campaigns. Pro: 5,000 actions/month, 500 AI replies, 3 accounts. Business: 20,000 actions/month, 2,000 AI replies, 6 accounts. Paid active campaigns are unlimited. One public reply or DM consumes one action; a reply plus DM consumes two. These are action allowances, not unique-contact allowances. Extra messages consume additional actions. Annual billing does not make usage annual. Internal account overrides are not public offers.

## Marketing priorities
Help visitors launch one working campaign, then measure activation and paid conversion. Seven locales are supported, including Arabic; keep new marketing UI explicitly localized and RTL-safe. No current funnel baseline or verified customer testimonial was established in this review. Do not raise Free limits or cut prices based only on competitor headline allowances; measure activation, retained use, delivery costs and upgrade behavior first.
